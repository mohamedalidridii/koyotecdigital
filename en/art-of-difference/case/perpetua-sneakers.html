Case study27/30 Streetwear

Perpetua Sneakers

Perpetual motion, in limited pairs.

A limited-edition sneaker brand sells a culture as much as an object. For Perpetua Sneakers, we imagined a site that stages products like a gallery, around one idea: perpetual motion.

The imagined brief

Perpetua makes sneakers in small runs, designed as design objects. Its community follows every drop and collects pairs. The site had to build anticipation around launches, tell the brand's world and pave the way for online sales.

The idea

The collection name, "Perpetual Motion", sits top left, simple and confident. Below, a series of images climbs like a staircase to the right — materials, architecture, glass objects, sculpture — like a movement that never stops. The brand tells its world before showing the product.

Art direction

A very light, almost mineral grey, deep black, mid greys and a bright red kept for accents and drops. A single typeface, PP Neue Montreal, sober and contemporary, leaves all the room to the images. The whole feels more like a design gallery than a sports shop.

Motion

The staircase images slide and change as you scroll, creating a continuous visual loop. Transitions are fluid and rhythmic, like a stride: the site invites you to keep scrolling to see what comes next.

The journey

An immersive brand page presenting the collection and its inspiration, which can then open onto product pages, drop dates and the store. The same visual language runs from campaign visuals to the cart.

What this concept brings to a real brand

For a fashion, accessories or limited-run brand, this concept shows how to build desirability before the sale. A strong world, then a fast, well-designed online store: that's the combination that turns anticipation into orders.

Concept: the brand and its brief were imagined by KOYOTEC to explore a creative direction. This is not a client project.
