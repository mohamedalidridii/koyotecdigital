KOYOTECThe founder Founder of KOYOTEC · Tech content creator

Mohamed Ali  Dridi 

I founded KOYOTEC on a simple belief: technology should be useful, beautiful and easy to understand. Alongside the agency, I explain tech simply to a community of more than 37,000 people on Instagram.

Tech, explained simply

Every week I publish short videos that make technology accessible: Linux, cloud, Docker and Kubernetes, databases, networking, DevOps, artificial intelligence. No needless jargon, concrete examples and a good dose of humour.

You'll find me on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, and sometimes live on Kick, coding and answering questions.

On stage

I also love sharing face to face. I speak at tech events and conferences, such as PyPAC. At a summit about the future, I presented "The Algorithm of Grief: End Game", a talk on how artificial intelligence — neural networks, language models — can help transform medical diagnosis.

On stage or on video, my goal stays the same: make complex technology clear for everyone.

Why KOYOTEC

Explaining tech to thousands of people taught me one thing: clarity always wins. That's what we apply at KOYOTEC, whether it's a website, a brand identity or a cloud architecture. A good project is fast, solid and understandable by the people who use it.

The agency brings design and engineering under one roof: websites, branding, SEO, and an Engineering team for AI, cloud, DevOps and cybersecurity.

Linux, open source and curiosity

I'm a Linux lover — and yes, I use Arch. I share my projects on GitHub and keep the same rule I started with: learn in public, test, fail, try again, and share what works.
