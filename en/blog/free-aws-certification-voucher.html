Free AWS certification in 2026: the official programs

2026-11-14By KOYOTEC

An AWS certification costs between 100 and 300 dollars. Yet AWS runs several official programs that let you take it for free or at half price. Here are the ones that exist in November 2026, who they are for, and what works from Tunisia and the Arab world.

How much does an AWS certification cost?

  • AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner: $100, 90 minutes, 65 questions
  • AWS Certified AI Practitioner: $100, 90 minutes, 65 questions
  • AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate: $150, 130 minutes, 65 questions
  • Professional and Specialty certifications: $300

You take the exam online, proctored remotely by Pearson VUE, or at a test centre. A certification is valid for 3 years. Details on the official AWS certification pages.

Urgent: the Emerging Talent Community closes on 9 December 2026

The AWS Emerging Talent Community (ETC) lets you earn points by completing training, then exchange them for rewards. AWS has announced it will close on 9 December 2026 at 00:00 UTC. Until then, members can still earn points and request rewards. After that, points are lost: they do not transfer.

To join, you must be 18 or older and hold a qualifying badge from AWS Educate, AWS re/Start or AWS Academy. If you are already a member, open the Rewards tab now: available rewards change with stock, so check whether exam vouchers are still there. Source: official AWS Educate FAQ.

AWS Educate: learn for free from age 13

AWS Educate is open to anyone aged 13 or older, with no credit card and no need to be a student. It offers online courses, hands-on labs (S3 storage, EC2 servers, VPC networking, RDS databases) and shareable digital badges. It is also where you earn the badges that open the ETC. Sign up on AWS Educate.

AWS Student Rewards: a $100 voucher for students

This AWS Builder Center program rewards verified students:

  • Completed profile: 1 year of AWS Skill Builder subscription
  • 7 badges: $10 in AWS credits
  • 14 badges: $20 in AWS credits
  • 21 badges: a $100 exam voucher, redeemable for AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner

You earn badges by taking part in Builder Center: publishing articles, commenting, keeping a sign-in streak. AWS removes low-quality content, so publish genuine write-ups.

Eligibility: 18 or older, enrolled in a higher education institution, and passing student verification (SheerID).

Note: Tunisia is among the countries excluded from the program for now, along with Syria, Iran and Cuba, among others. Morocco, Algeria, Egypt, the Gulf states and Europe are not on that list. Source: official Builder Center FAQ, program page: Student Rewards.

AWS re/Start: free training for a career change

re/Start is a free program for people aged 18 or older who are unemployed or underemployed. No technical background is required. Local partner organisations deliver the training and, according to AWS, it includes access to practice exams and certification exams. Cohorts have already run in Tunisia, for example with Orange Tunisie in 2022. Open cohorts by country are listed on the How to apply to re/Start page.

AWS Student Builder Groups (formerly Cloud Clubs)

AWS Cloud Clubs are now called AWS Student Builder Groups. They are student clubs backed by AWS, open to post-secondary students aged 18 or older. Group leaders receive AWS credits, certification exam vouchers and swag. No group is listed in Tunisia yet: any student can apply to start one at their university. See Student Builder Groups.

AWS Academy: ask your university

AWS Academy is for institutions, not individuals. Students who take an AWS Academy course get 12 months of Skill Builder subscription. If your school is not a member, talk to a lecturer: the institution applies. Details on AWS Academy.

After your first certification: 50% off the next one

Once certified, you get a 50% discount voucher for your next AWS exam or your recertification, in your AWS Certification account. The smart order: a free Foundational certification first, then Solutions Architect – Associate for $75 instead of $150. Source: AWS certification benefits.

Prepare for free with AWS Skill Builder

AWS Skill Builder offers more than 900 free online courses, including the official exam preparation courses. The paid subscription ($29 per month) unlocks the full set of exam prep resources and hands-on exercises. With Student Rewards or AWS Academy, you get it free for 12 months. See AWS Skill Builder.

What about the program for AWS partners?

You may have heard of 100% vouchers through "AWS Partner Certification Readiness". That program is reserved for employees of AWS partner companies. You register with the company's work email, which is how AWS checks eligibility. At KOYOTEC, an AWS partner, our team is taking the November 2026 sessions. If you work for an AWS partner, ask your company. Otherwise, the programs above are the official route.

The fastest plan

  • Before 9 December: if you hold an AWS Educate, re/Start or Academy badge, use your ETC points.
  • Create an AWS Educate account and take the free courses and labs.
  • Prepare Cloud Practitioner or AI Practitioner with the free Skill Builder courses.
  • Student outside Tunisia: aim for the 21 Student Rewards badges and the $100 voucher.
  • Student in Tunisia: start a Student Builder Group at your university.
  • Unemployed: watch for the next re/Start cohorts in your country.
  • Once certified: use the 50% voucher for Solutions Architect – Associate.

Information checked on AWS's official pages on 14 November 2026. Programs change: reread the conditions before you sign up.

Frequently asked questions

Can you take an AWS certification for free?

Yes, through official programs: the $100 AWS Student Rewards voucher for verified students, Emerging Talent Community rewards until 9 December 2026, AWS re/Start for unemployed people, and Student Builder Groups for their leaders.

Is the AWS Cloud Practitioner certification free?

No, the exam costs $100. But the $100 AWS Student Rewards voucher covers exactly the Cloud Practitioner exam, and the Skill Builder preparation courses are free.

Is AWS Student Rewards available in Tunisia?

Not for now. The Builder Center FAQ lists Tunisia among the excluded countries. From Tunisia, use AWS Educate, Skill Builder, re/Start and Student Builder Groups.

When does the AWS Emerging Talent Community close?

On 9 December 2026 at 00:00 UTC. Points and rewards do not transfer to AWS Builder Center, so request your rewards before then.

Which AWS certification should I take first?

Cloud Practitioner for cloud basics, or AI Practitioner if you are aiming for AI. After that, the 50% voucher makes Solutions Architect – Associate much cheaper.
