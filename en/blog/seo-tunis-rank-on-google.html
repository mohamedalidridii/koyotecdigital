SEO in Tunis: how to rank on Google

2026-10-17 · By KOYOTEC

Reaching the first page of Google takes steady work, not a trick. These are the steps that matter for a business based in Tunis.

1. Choose the right queries

Think like your customer. They type "website design Tunis" or "branding agency Tunisia", not industry jargon. List these phrases, in French, Arabic and English depending on your audience, and use them in your titles and texts.

2. One page per service

Each important service deserves its own page with a clear title, a unique description and useful content. Avoid putting everything on one page.

3. Get the technical side right

  • A fast site, especially on mobile
  • Unique title and description tags on every page
  • A sitemap submitted in Google Search Console
  • One official address (with or without www); the others redirect to it

4. Create a Google Business Profile

To show up in local searches and on Google Maps, create and complete your profile: address, phone, opening hours, photos, description. Ask satisfied customers for reviews.

5. Publish useful content

Articles that answer your customers' questions attract qualified visitors. Publish regularly and link your articles to your service pages.

6. Earn quality links

Links from other reputable sites (business directories, partners, local press) tell Google your site is trustworthy. They come gradually.

How long does it take?

Expect several weeks before the first effects, and often several months for competitive queries. Nobody can guarantee a specific position on Google.
