Website cost in Tunisia in 2026: how much does a website cost?

2026-10-17By KOYOTEC

At KOYOTEC, websites in Tunisia start from 800 TND. The final price depends mostly on the type of site (showcase, online store, custom platform), the number of pages, the level of custom design, the features, the languages and how much copy needs writing. This guide explains each cost item, the costs to plan for after launch and how to read a quote, so you can compare offers without surprises.

Website cost in Tunisia at a glance

  • Starting point at KOYOTEC: websites from 800 TND.
  • Final price: set in a free, itemised quote with no commitment.
  • Reply time: within 24 hours. For a complex project, we first suggest a short call to understand your needs.
  • What raises the budget: number of pages, custom design, online payment, languages, copywriting and specific features.
  • After launch: the domain name, hosting and, if you want it, maintenance.

A price on its own says very little. Two quotes for the same amount can cover very different work, and two very different quotes may describe two sites that have nothing in common. The rest of this article helps you understand exactly what you are paying for.

What drives the price of a website

The cost of a website mostly reflects the time needed to design, build, test and launch it. These are the factors that weigh most on that time, and therefore on the price:

  • Type of site: a few-page showcase site, an online store and a platform with customer accounts involve very different amounts of work.
  • Number of pages: every page has to be structured, designed, written, built and optimized. One page per service is often best for Google, but it adds volume.
  • Design level: an adapted template costs less than a design drawn for your brand.
  • Features: quote form, booking, appointments, client area, catalogue, online payment, connection to other software.
  • Languages: each language version means extra copy, settings and checks.
  • Content: copy to write, photos to shoot or select, products to enter.
  • SEO: built in from the start or left out.
  • Admin: being able to edit your own text, images and news.

The next sections cover the points that make quotes in Tunisia vary the most.

Custom design or template: what is the price difference?

A template (ready-made theme) starts from an existing layout adapted to your colours and copy. It is the fastest option, so the cheapest. Its limit: your site looks like many others, and the structure is not always built around your customers or your keywords.

A custom design starts from your business: who your customers are, what they look for and what you want them to do (call, ask for a quote, book, buy). The structure, mock-ups and copy are designed for that goal, on mobile and desktop. It takes more design time, but it is often what makes a brand memorable and a website effective.

At KOYOTEC, we don't start from a bought theme with new colours: every project in our web design in Tunisia service is drawn for the business and its customers. If you don't have a logo or brand guidelines yet, we can create your brand identity alongside the website.

E-commerce and online payment in Tunisia

An online store costs more than a showcase site because it does far more: catalogue, product pages, cart, checkout, confirmation emails, stock and order management, delivery zones and rates.

Payment is a cost item of its own. In Tunisia, cash on delivery is still very common, and card payment in dinars goes through an online payment gateway, usually linked to your bank. Integration depends on your bank and your business. If you sell abroad, international payment is added to the list.

The budget for an e-commerce website therefore depends mostly on catalogue size, payment and delivery options, features (variants, customer accounts, loyalty, multiple languages) and design level. A small store with a few dozen products and a platform with thousands of items do not cost the same.

Multilingual websites: French, Arabic, English

Many Tunisian businesses address French-speaking, Arabic-speaking and international customers at the same time. Each added language needs translated or adapted copy, page titles and descriptions for Google in that language, and correct hreflang tags so search engines show the right version to the right visitor.

Arabic needs specific work: it is written right to left, so the layout has to be adapted, not just the text. Plan it from the start rather than adding it later: it is simpler and cheaper. KOYOTEC builds websites in French, Arabic and English.

SEO: included or extra?

A website that does not show up on Google does not bring you customers. A quote should separate two things:

  • SEO foundations, which are part of the build: page structure, titles and descriptions, clean URLs, sitemap, structured data, loading speed and mobile version. These are not extras.
  • Ongoing SEO: rank tracking, new content and continuous page improvements. This is a separate service that continues after launch.

If a quote mentions none of this, ask why. To go further, read our guide to ranking on Google in Tunis or see our SEO services in Tunisia.

Price by type of website: showcase, e-commerce, custom, redesign

Here is what each type of project usually covers, and what makes its price vary. The starting point at KOYOTEC is 800 TND for a website; for each project, the exact amount is set in the quote.

  • Showcase website: a few pages presenting your business, services, references and contact details, with a contact or quote form. This is the most affordable format. The price varies with the number of pages, the design and the languages.
  • Company website: a fuller site with a page per service, references, a blog and several forms. The volume of pages and copy makes the difference.
  • E-commerce website: catalogue, cart, online or cash-on-delivery payment, order and delivery management. The price depends on the catalogue, payment options and features.
  • Custom website or web app: booking, client area, appointments, dashboard, connection to your tools. This is the most variable format, since each feature is built for your needs.
  • Redesign: your site exists but is slow, dated or poorly ranked. We analyse what works, keep it, and set up clean redirects so you don't lose your search rankings. The price depends on how many pages need reworking and what can be kept.

Why not publish a fixed price list per type of site? Because a "showcase site" can mean four pages in one language or twenty pages in three. An honest price starts from your actual project.

Recurring costs: domain, hosting, maintenance

The build price is not the only budget to plan. Three items come back after launch:

  • Domain name: renewed every year. A .tn domain is registered through an accredited registrar and is reserved for Tunisian nationals or legal residents and organisations, and for foreign companies with a valid trademark in Tunisia, according to the official .tn registry run by the Tunisian Internet Agency (ATI). A .com or another extension is also possible.
  • Hosting: the server that makes your website available. Its cost depends on the type of site, traffic and expected performance. An online store has different needs from a showcase site.
  • Maintenance: updates, security, backups, small changes, new pages. It can be occasional or regular, depending on your needs.

One essential point: the domain name should be registered in your name. At KOYOTEC, we advise you on the right domain and hosting, we handle the launch, and the domain stays in your name.

How to read a website quote

A good quote lets you compare offers and know exactly what you are buying. Check that it states:

  • the list of pages and features;
  • the type of design (adapted template or custom) and the number of mock-ups or revision rounds;
  • who writes the copy and provides the photos;
  • the languages included;
  • the SEO work included in the build;
  • the mobile version and the admin interface, with training;
  • what is included for the domain and hosting, and what is billed separately;
  • maintenance terms after launch;
  • the schedule, step by step.

This is why quotes vary so much: agencies don't price the same scope. Compare line by line, not just the total.

Red flags in a website quote

  • A single total with no breakdown of pages or features.
  • A domain registered in the provider's name instead of yours.
  • No access to the site's admin, or every text change billed.
  • No mention of SEO, speed or the mobile version.
  • Yearly costs (hosting, maintenance, licences) that only appear after signing.
  • A bought theme presented as a custom design.
  • No schedule and no approval steps.
  • A promise of the top spot on Google: nobody can honestly guarantee it.

How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends mostly on three things: the number of pages, the features and how quickly content is approved on your side. A store with online payment also needs time to set up the gateway with your bank. At KOYOTEC, a project runs in five steps:

  • Brief: understanding your business, customers, competitors and goals.
  • Structure and content: site map, pages, keywords and copy.
  • Mock-ups: design of the key pages, on mobile and desktop, refined with you until approved.
  • Development: build, forms, admin, speed, technical SEO and testing on several devices.
  • Launch and follow-up: domain, hosting, Search Console, training, then support.

We give you a detailed schedule with the quote, step by step, and keep it updated during the project. The best way to move fast: prepare your copy, photos and approvals in advance.
