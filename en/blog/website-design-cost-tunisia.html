How much does a website cost in Tunisia?

2026-10-17 · By KOYOTEC

Website prices in Tunisia vary a lot from one project to another. These are the factors that really make the difference, so you can compare quotes properly.

The type of site

A simple showcase site has a different budget from an online store with payments or a platform with customer accounts. The more pages, features and content to integrate, the more work is involved.

Design: template or custom

A ready-made template costs less but looks like many other sites. A custom design takes more time because it starts from your identity and your customers. It is often what makes a brand memorable.

Features

  • Contact or quote request form
  • Online payment and order management
  • Multilingual site (French, Arabic, English)
  • An admin area so you can edit content yourself

SEO

A site that does not show up on Google does not bring you customers. Check that the quote includes page structure, tags, loading speed and a mobile version. These are basics of search optimization, not extras.

Costs after launch

Plan for hosting, a domain name, maintenance and updates. Ask what is included in the quote and what is billed separately.

How to get a reliable quote

Describe your business, your goals and the pages you need. The more precise your request, the more precise the quote. At KOYOTEC, quotes are free.
