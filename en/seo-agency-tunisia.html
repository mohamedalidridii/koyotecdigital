Search engine optimization  (SEO) in Tunisia 

Audit, technical work, content and local SEO: a methodical approach so customers find you on Google when they search for what you offer.

Request a free audit→

When customers search for a service in Tunis, they almost always click one of Google's first results. Search engine optimization, or SEO, means making your website clear, fast and useful enough to earn those places. KOYOTEC supports Tunisian businesses with their SEO, from the first audit to month-by-month follow-up.

How Google ranking works

Google tries to show the page that best answers each search. To do that it looks at three main things:

  • technical health: can your site be crawled, understood and shown quickly, on mobile and desktop?
  • content: do your pages answer exactly what your customers search for, in their words?
  • reputation: do other sites, directories and customer reviews talk about you?

Good SEO works on all three pillars at once. There is no single trick and no lasting shortcut.

SEO audit: knowing where you start

Every engagement starts with an audit of your site and your market:

  • technical state: indexing, errors, duplicate pages, redirects, speed, mobile version;
  • keywords: what your customers actually type into Google, in French, Arabic or English, and how often;
  • competition: who ranks in your place today, and why;
  • content: missing pages, pages to rewrite, pages competing with each other.

You receive a clear report with a list of actions ranked by impact.

Technical SEO

We fix what stops Google from crawling and understanding your site: sitemap, canonical tags, redirects, error pages, hreflang for multilingual sites, structured data and performance (Core Web Vitals). These fixes are often invisible to visitors but decisive for visibility.

Content and service pages

Most business websites put all their services on a single page. As a result, no page truly answers a specific search. We build one page per service and search intent with you, with titles, copy and FAQs written for your customers, then blog posts that answer their questions before they buy.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

For a search like "web agency Tunis" or "dentist Ennasr", Google first shows a map with three businesses. To appear there you need a complete, verified Google Business Profile, identical details everywhere (name, address, phone), regular customer reviews and a website that confirms your location. We help you set all of this up and keep it active.

Follow-up and results

SEO is long-term work: first effects usually appear after a few weeks, and rankings consolidate over several months, depending on competition in your sector. Nobody can honestly guarantee the top spot.

What we do guarantee is transparent work: monthly reporting on your rankings, visits and contact requests from Google Search Console and your analytics, with the actions taken and the next steps.

SEO FAQ

(06)
01How soon will I see results?

First improvements often appear after a few weeks, especially after technical fixes. For competitive searches, expect several months of regular work.

02Can you guarantee first place on Google?

No, and nobody honestly can: Google alone decides its rankings. We commit to the method, transparency and tracking results.

03Is SEO worth it for a small local business?

Yes, that's often where it pays off most. Local SEO and a well-kept Google Business Profile can be enough to appear ahead of competitors in your area or city.

04Do you work on websites you didn't build?

Yes. We start with an audit of your current site, whatever the technology, then propose the possible fixes or, if needed, a redesign.

05What's the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

Google Ads shows paid ads for as long as you pay. SEO works on the organic results: it takes longer, but visits keep coming without paying for each click.

06Do you do SEO in Arabic and English?

Yes. We work on keywords and pages in your customers' language, with a correct multilingual structure for Google.
