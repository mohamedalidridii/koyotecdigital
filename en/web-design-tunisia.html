A website is not an online brochure. It is often the first contact between your business and a customer searching on Google. KOYOTEC is a web agency in Ennasr, Tunis, designing and building custom websites for companies, shops and freelancers in Tunisia and abroad.

Which website does your business need?

Every project starts with one question: what should your website do? Depending on the answer, we build:

  • a showcase website of a few pages presenting your business, services and contact details;
  • a fuller company website with a page per service, references, a blog and forms;
  • a personal website or portfolio for a freelancer, consultant or creative;
  • a web platform with custom features: booking, client area, catalogue, appointments;
  • an online store to sell in Tunisia and abroad.

We don't start from a bought theme with new colours. The structure, design and content are drawn for your business and your customers.

Fast, responsive and easy to manage

Most visits come from a phone. Every website we deliver adapts to mobile, tablet and desktop, and is optimized to load quickly, even on an average 4G connection.

A slow website loses visitors before they see your offer, and Google ranks it lower. So we work on image weight, font loading, code and hosting to reach good Core Web Vitals scores.

Once live, you should be able to edit your text, photos and news without waiting for a developer. We set up a simple admin interface and show you how to use it.

Built for Google from day one

A beautiful website that nobody finds is not much use. Search engine optimization (SEO) is part of the build, not an add-on at the end:

  • one page per service and per search intent, with titles and descriptions written for Google;
  • clean page URLs, a sitemap and canonical tags;
  • structured data for your business, services and FAQs;
  • French, English and Arabic versions if your customers need them, with correct hreflang tags;
  • Google Search Console and Google Business Profile set up so you can track results.

We write or rework your copy with you so it answers the questions your customers actually ask.

Design and identity: a website that looks like you

Your website should be recognisable at a glance. If you already have a logo and brand guidelines, we carry them onto the web. If not, we can create your visual identity alongside the website so everything fits together: logo, colours, typefaces, photography and tone.

The design serves one goal: getting the visitor to call you, ask for a quote, book or buy. Every page is built around that action.

How much does a website cost in Tunisia?

The price depends mostly on the number of pages, the level of custom design, the features (booking, payment, client area), the number of languages and how much copy needs writing. A simple showcase site and a platform with online payment have very different budgets.

After a first conversation, we send you a free, itemised quote so you know exactly what you are paying for. To plan your budget, also read our guide to website design costs in Tunisia on the blog.

After launch

A website keeps evolving. We stay available after launch for updates, new pages, security and performance monitoring. We can also support your SEO over time: ranking analysis, new content and continuous improvement of the pages that convert.

Website design FAQ

(06)
01How long does it take to build a website?

It depends mostly on the number of pages, the features and how quickly content is approved. We give you a detailed schedule with the quote, step by step, and keep it updated during the project.

02Will I be able to edit my website myself?

Yes. We set up a simple admin interface to edit your text, images and news, and we train you on it at handover.

03Will my website rank on Google?

The website is built to SEO best practice: structure, tags, speed, structured data and content written for your keywords. Rankings also depend on competition and ongoing work; we can support you with that follow-up.

04Do you handle the domain name and hosting?

We advise you on the domain name (.tn, .com or other) and the right hosting for your site, and we take care of the launch. The domain stays in your name.

05Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We review your current site, keep what works and set up clean redirects so you don't lose your search rankings during the redesign.

06Do you only work in Tunis?

We are based in Ennasr, Tunis, and work with clients across Tunisia and abroad. Most of the collaboration can happen remotely.
