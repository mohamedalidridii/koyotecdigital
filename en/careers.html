Hiring FAQ

(06)
01Do you offer internships?

Yes. Every year we welcome interns and end-of-studies projects (PFE), on the Digital and the Engineering side. Choose “Internship” and mention the period in your message.

02Do I need a cover letter?

No. Your CV and a few lines on what drives you are enough. A portfolio, a GitHub or side projects often count more than a letter.

03Is remote work possible?

We are based in Ennasr, Tunis. Depending on the role we offer on-site, hybrid or remote work, especially for freelancers.

04Which languages do you work in?

French, Arabic and English. Our clients are in Tunisia and abroad, so good technical English is a real plus.

05When will I hear back?

We read every application and answer within 10 working days. If no role fits today, we keep your profile for upcoming projects.

06What happens to my CV?

It is only shared with the KOYOTEC hiring team, kept for 12 months and then deleted. You can ask for its deletion at any time at careers@koyotec.digital.
