The project

Little Lanterns serves children affected by crisis and displacement, offering them comfort through audio stories.

What she did

Working in a crisis-communication capacity, Nour led the implementation of mental health and psychosocial support (MHPSS), woven into creative audio storytelling.

Impact

The programme brought comforting stories to children in conflict zones, offering emotional relief, learning and moments of imaginative escape. It received the Horizon Award for Reaching Underserved Audiences.
