Nour El Kamel · Tunis

About

Nour El Kamel

Senior Projects Manager at Horizon Media Trust, based in Tunis. Over a decade using media and storytelling to drive social change across North Africa, the Middle East and beyond.

01 — Story

Storytelling as connection, empathy and empowerment.

Background

I've spent more than ten years in international development, NGO management and social change work across North Africa, the Middle East and beyond. Throughout, I've focused on empowering communities, building partnerships and driving sustainable development through innovative, evidence-based programs.

I studied at the Institut Méditerranéen de la Communication, and my career has taken me from countering violent extremism and youth debate in Tunisia to leading multi-country media programmes for Horizon Media Trust.

What drives me

My work combines strategic thinking, innovative communication and deep community engagement to design programs that change knowledge, attitudes and behaviour. I'm committed to advancing the UN Sustainable Development Goals, especially education, gender equality and sustainable economic development.
