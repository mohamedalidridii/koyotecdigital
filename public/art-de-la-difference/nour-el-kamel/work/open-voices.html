The story behind it.

The initiative

This work reflects Nour's core professional philosophy: accurate, credible and ethical information is a fundamental human right, and a structural lifeline during humanitarian emergencies.

What she does

Using Communication for Development (C4D) frameworks, Nour bridges the gap between fragile communities, international NGOs and governments, including the Tunisian and Algerian governments.

In Mauritania, she works with national broadcasters on audience research, digital media literacy and public-interest storytelling.
