Manifesto

A gown is built like a confidence: stitch by stitch, by hand, in the quiet of the atelier. Silk, velvet and gold take the time they need — time you cannot see, but can feel.

The name
GoldAurum
Faithfulnessfidèle
Aurèle

“Aurèle” is born of two words: aurum, Latin for gold, and fidélité — the Maison's faithfulness to the couture gesture, passed from hand to hand.

The collection

Couture, in the light of gold

At the heart of the season runs a single thread: gold. Embroidered, woven, set as beads or buttons, it lights silhouettes sculpted in silk and velvet.

Signature

Gold, set like jewellery

Gold-thread embroidery, beading, monogrammed buttons: every detail is made by hand to catch the light, never to steal it.

The atelier

A thousand hours, one thread

Every piece begins as a sketch, then a toile, then hundreds of hours of handwork. The atelier is not after speed: it is after precision.
