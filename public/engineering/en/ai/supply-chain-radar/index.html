Mirvane Pharma

Predictive supply disruption radar

Seeing supplier delays coming before they stop production

In short

The system matches open purchase orders against world news (port strikes, weather, authority notices): as soon as an event threatens a supplier, it finds the affected orders, estimates the delay and proposes a solution to procurement.

Client
Mirvane Pharma
Sector
Pharmaceutical manufacturing
Size
800 staff, 120 international suppliers
  • 9 daysaverage warning ahead of a supplier delay
  • −40%line stops caused by supply issues
  • 24/7monitoring of 120 suppliers

The context

Mirvane Pharma imports its active ingredients and packaging from Asia and Europe. Until now, procurement found out about a delay when the delivery didn’t arrive, often with production lines standing still.

The challenge

  1. 01

    Warning signs exist (announced strikes, typhoons, port congestion), but no one can follow them all.

  2. 02

    A distant event has to be linked to the actual orders it threatens.

  3. 03

    One false alarm too many, and teams stop trusting the system.

  4. 04

    The answer must be actionable: what to do, not only what to fear.

Our approach

Open orders, suppliers and their routes are synced from the ERP. Scheduled jobs continuously collect news, weather alerts and port notices, analysed by a RAG pipeline that only keeps events touching a tracked place or route. The system estimates the likely delay, lists the affected orders and drafts an alert with alternatives (another port, another qualified supplier).

The solution, step by step

  1. 01

    Your open orders

    Each order is linked to its supplier, its port and its route.

  2. 02

    The world, watched

    News, weather and port notices are analysed continuously, day and night.

  3. 03

    An event detected

    A strike is announced at the port of Genoa from the 12th.

  4. 04

    The impact, quantified

    Two orders are affected; the likely delay is estimated at 9 days.

  5. 05

    An alert with solutions

    Procurement gets the alert and an alternative: route through Marseille.

Results

  • 9 daysaverage warning ahead of delays
  • −40%production stops caused by supply
  • 85%of alerts rated useful by buyers
  • 120suppliers monitored continuously

What we deliver

  • Order and supplier sync from the ERP
  • Scheduled collection: news, weather, port notices
  • RAG pipeline for event detection and matching
  • Delay estimates and suggested alternatives
  • React dashboard and procurement alerts

Technologies

  • Python
  • Celery
  • Redis
  • Pinecone
  • News API
  • React

Timeline

8 to 10 weeks

Team

1 AI engineer, 1 full-stack developer
