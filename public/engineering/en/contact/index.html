FAQ / 01

Common questions

Your questions answered

  1. 01

    What kind of projects do you take on?

    Applied AI, cloud, DevOps, cybersecurity and business intelligence.

    That ranges from an AI assistant wired to your documents to a full infrastructure migration, a security audit or a data warehouse.

  2. 02

    How do you price an engagement?

    After a free first call, we propose a scope and a fixed price for audits and projects.

    Ongoing support (run) is billed monthly, with a set number of hours.

  3. 03

    Which cloud providers do you work with?

    AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, as well as local hosting providers and on-premise servers.

    We recommend what fits your budget and constraints, not a particular vendor.

  4. 04

    How do you protect our data?

    We sign an NDA before any access, use named accounts with multi-factor authentication and least privilege, and log every access we are given.

    Your data stays in your environment whenever possible, and we work on anonymised or test data when the task allows it.

  5. 05

    Do you work with EU companies under the GDPR?

    Yes. When we handle personal data on your behalf, we sign a data processing agreement (Article 28 GDPR) before work starts.

    We are based in Tunisia, outside the EU, so any access from our team is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

    Production data can stay in EU hosting (for example AWS Paris or Frankfurt) or on your own servers: we deploy where your compliance requires.

  6. 06

    What happens after delivery?

    Every project ends with full documentation and a handover to your teams.

    If you want, we keep monitoring and improving the system under a run contract.
