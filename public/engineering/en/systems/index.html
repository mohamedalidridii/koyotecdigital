AI

An assistant that knows all your documents

For whom
A 200-person company whose procedures, contracts and internal notes sleep in thousands of files.
The problem
Finding the right information takes hours, and newcomers keep asking the same questions.

What we build

  1. 01

    Your documents

    PDFs, Word files, emails, wiki: we gather them without taking them out of your infrastructure.

  2. 02

    Indexed by meaning

    Each passage becomes a point in a space where related ideas cluster together.

  3. 03

    A question, in plain words

    Your teams ask their question as they would ask a colleague.

  4. 04

    The right passages

    The assistant finds the most relevant excerpts, not just keywords.

  5. 05

    An answer with sources

    The answer cites its sources: you can always check.

What it means for you

  • A few seconds

    instead of long searches through shared folders

  • Sources cited

    in every answer, so trust stays

  • Your data stays yours

    hosted on your servers or in Europe

Technologies

  • Python
  • LangChain
  • PostgreSQL + pgvector
  • Open-source LLM or API
  • Docker

Typical timeline

6 to 10 weeks

DevOps

From code to production, no downtime, no stress

For whom
A product team that deploys by hand, on a Friday night, fingers crossed.
The problem
Every release is slow, risky, and depends on a single person.

What we build

  1. 01

    Code is pushed

    Every change triggers the chain, with no human step.

  2. 02

    Built, tested, scanned

    Compilation, automated tests and security scan, every single time.

  3. 03

    Packaged once and for all

    The app becomes a versioned container image, identical everywhere.

  4. 04

    Deployed with no downtime

    Kubernetes replaces old versions one by one: your users notice nothing.

  5. 05

    Watched continuously

    Real-time metrics and alerts, automatic rollback when needed.

What it means for you

  • In minutes

    releases several times a day

  • Zero downtime

    during deployments

  • Rollback

    in one click, or automatic

« Harbor »

Cloud

An infrastructure that holds the load

For whom
An online shop hosted on a single server that goes down at every sale.
The problem
At the slightest traffic peak, the site slows down then stops, and every minute costs sales.

What we build

  1. 01

    Today: a single server

    Everything relies on one machine: if it falls, everything stops.

  2. 02

    Described as code

    The whole infrastructure is written in Terraform: reproducible, versioned, reviewable.

  3. 03

    Spread over three zones

    Servers and database are duplicated in separate data centres.

  4. 04

    It follows the traffic

    During a peak, new servers start on their own, then shut down.

  5. 05

    Backed up, costs under control

    Automatic backups and a budget tracked every month.

What it means for you

  • 99.9%

    target availability

  • Automatic

    scaling during peaks

  • Tracked bill

    optimised every month

Technologies

  • AWS
  • Azure
  • Google Cloud
  • Terraform
  • Kubernetes
  • Managed PostgreSQL
  • CDN

Typical timeline

6 to 12 weeks

Cybersecurity

Find the flaws before attackers do

For whom
An SME handling customer data without knowing where its weak spots are.
The problem
One flaw can be enough: data theft, ransomware, days of downtime.

What we build

  1. 01

    We look for the flaws

    We test your system the way an attacker would, with your consent.

  2. 02

    A clear report

    Every flaw ranked by severity, explained simply, with its fix.

  3. 03

    We close the doors

    Updates, access, passwords, firewall: the attack surface shrinks.

  4. 04

    24/7 monitoring

    Intrusion attempts are detected and blocked in real time.

  5. 05

    Your teams trained

    Phishing awareness: your people are the first line of defence.

What it means for you

  • Clear priorities

    critical flaws fixed first

  • Real-time alerts

    instead of finding out weeks later

  • Easier compliance

    ISO 27001, GDPR, Tunisian data law

Technologies

  • OWASP ZAP
  • Nmap
  • Burp Suite
  • Wazuh
  • CrowdSec
  • MFA

Typical timeline

Audit in 2 to 3 weeks, then monthly follow-up

Data & BI

All your data, one dashboard

For whom
A distributor whose figures live in the ERP, three Excel files and the CRM.
The problem

What we build

  1. 01

    Scattered data

    Sales in the ERP, stock in Excel, customers in the CRM: nothing talks.

  2. 02

    Collected every night

    Automated pipelines extract, clean and match the data.

  3. 03

    One warehouse

    A single source of truth, historised and secured.

  4. 04

    Live dashboards

    Revenue, margins, stock: up to date every morning, on desktop or mobile.

  5. 05

    Useful alerts

    The information arrives before the problem costs money.

What it means for you

  • No more exports

    copied by hand into Excel

  • Fresh every morning

    with no one involved

  • Decisions

    made on reliable figures

Technologies

  • Airbyte

  • Metabase
